and low vision

Make worksheets easy to read – the layout stays. CVI PDF resets the text in a clear font with more spacing. Images, tables and lines stay exactly where they are.

    Verdana or Arial instead of ornate or narrow fonts.

CVI PDF

Three
steps

  1. Open

    Choose a PDF from Files, open a photo or scan printed pages.

  2. Adjust

    Choose font, line spacing and letter spacing. The preview shows the result instantly.

  3. Save

    The new PDF is called Name_CVI.pdf and goes to Files – ready for the child’s iPad.

More
apps

Two more tools with the same goal: being able to do more on your own.

  • Tastenreise

    Learn to type

    The keyboard becomes familiar. A touch-typing trainer for children with and without visual impairment – on a real keyboard connected to the iPad. 16 levels, read-aloud, VoiceOver and braille display, fully keyboard-operated. Currently in German.

    • 16 levels
    • Read-aloud
    • VoiceOver & braille

  • PDF Text Field

    Create forms

    From worksheet to fillable PDF. The tool for teachers detects writing lines, boxes and checkboxes and turns them into real form fields – so children can type instead of writing by hand. Currently in German.

    • Detect fields
    • Fine-tune
    • Save as form

About
me

My name is Philipp and I live in Zurich.

I am a special needs educator working with children and young people with visual impairment. Today I lead a team that supports and advises these pupils in their schools.

In everyday work I often see where things get stuck. A worksheet a child cannot fill in on the iPad. A text set too tightly. A program that only works with an account or the internet.

My apps grow out of moments like these. They are made for people who see poorly or not at all, and for the professionals who work with them.
