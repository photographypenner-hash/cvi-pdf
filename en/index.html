Sichtnah

Apps for people with
visual impairments

sichtnah makes learning and working more accessible – with small, thoughtful apps for iPad and Mac. For people who see poorly or not at all, and for the professionals who work with them.

Close to
everyday life

Every app grows out of a situation I come across while working with children and young people with visual impairments: a worksheet set too tightly, a form that cannot be filled in on the iPad, a keyboard that still feels unfamiliar.

  • 01

    Easy to perceive

    Clear fonts, calm surfaces and plenty of space – designed for people with visual impairments and CVI.

  • 02

    For all senses

    Usable with VoiceOver, a Braille display and the keyboard, not only with the eyes.

  • 03

    Simple in daily use

    Few steps, no sign-in. The apps should save time, not cost it.

Three
apps

Three tools with the same goal: being able to do more on your own.

  • CVI PDF

    Make worksheets easy to read

    Resets the text of PDF worksheets in a clear font with more spacing. Images, tables and lines stay exactly where they are – for children with CVI and low vision.

    • Clear fonts
    • More space
    • Layout stays

  • Tastenreise

    Learn to type

    Get familiar with the keyboard. A touch-typing trainer for children with and without visual impairments – on a physical keyboard connected to an iPad, with spoken guidance, VoiceOver and Braille display.

    • 16 levels
    • Spoken guidance
    • VoiceOver & Braille

  • PDF Text Field

    Create PDF forms

    Turn a worksheet into a fillable PDF. Detects writing lines, boxes and checkboxes and turns them into real form fields – so children can type instead of writing by hand.

    • Detect fields
    • Review and edit
    • Save as a form

About
me

My name is Philipp. I live in Zurich and I am a special needs educator. I work with children and young people with visual impairments and lead a team that supports and advises these pupils in their schools.

The apps of sichtnah grow out of what I see in this work.
