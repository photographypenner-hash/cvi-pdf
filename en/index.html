CVI PDF

Makes PDF worksheets easy to read for children with cerebral visual impairment (CVI) – on iPad and Mac.

  • Readable fontsVerdana, Arial or the original font, at 14, 16 or 18 points.
  • More space1.5× or 2× line spacing and more space between letters.
  • Calm pagesImages on their own line, tables with a clear grid, colours such as yellow on black.
  • Scanned sheetsScan printed pages with the iPad camera; text recognition reads the text.
  • Accessible PDFReal text with headings, paragraphs, tables and image descriptions for VoiceOver.
  • Everything stays on your deviceNo sign-in, no ads, no data on the internet.

How it works

  1. Open a PDF, choose a photo or scan pages.
  2. Adjust font, spacing, images and colours. The preview shows the result instantly.
  3. Save – the new PDF is called Name_CVI.pdf and goes to Files.
