CVIPDF

For children with CVI
and low vision

Make worksheets easy to read – the layout stays. CVI PDF resets the text in a clear font with more spacing. Images, tables and lines stay exactly where they are.

Only the text
gets better

  • 01

    Clear fonts

    Verdana or Arial instead of ornate or narrow fonts.

  • 02

    More space

    1.5× or 2× line spacing and more space between letters.

  • 03

    Layout stays

    Every text stays in its place. Images, tables, lines and boxes are not changed.

  • 04

    Printed sheets too

    Scan pages with the iPad camera – text recognition reads the text.

  • 05

    Accessible PDF

    Real text with headings, paragraphs and image descriptions for VoiceOver.

  • 06

    Everything stays on the device

    No sign-in, no ads, no data on the internet.
