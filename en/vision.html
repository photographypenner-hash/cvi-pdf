Vision

Where sichtnah
is heading

Children and young people with visual impairments learn with the same materials as their class – independently and without detours.

What
drives me

Good assistive tools hardly stand out in everyday life. They are simply there when you need them. That is what sichtnah aims for.

  • 01

    Learning independently

    Children with visual impairments should be able to work with their materials without waiting for help.

  • 02

    Accessible from the start

    Worksheets, forms and tools should work for all senses – not only after being adapted.

  • 03

    Relief for professionals

    Teachers and specialists should spend less time adapting materials and more time with the children.

  • 04

    Data stays with the child

    Local, offline, no AI: what children work on belongs on their device and nowhere else.

Thinking
further

sichtnah grows with the questions of everyday life. Is a tool missing, or do you have an idea? Write to me.
