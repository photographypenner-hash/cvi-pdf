PDF Text Field Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

In short: PDF Text Field collects no data. Everything stays on your device. The app needs no internet and has no account, no ads, no AI and no analytics software.

Who is responsible?

Philipp Penner, Sichtnah, Zurich
Email: support@sichtnah.com

PDF files

You choose a PDF file yourself. The app only reads this one file. The search for writing spaces runs entirely on your device, without internet and without artificial intelligence. The opened PDF stays in memory only while it is open. The app does not change the original file.

Save

A new PDF file with form fields is only created when you tap “Sichern” (Save). You decide where it is stored in the save dialog. The storage and privacy rules of that location apply, for example iCloud Drive.

What the app remembers

Nothing. The app stores no settings and no information about how you use it.

Sharing with others

The app does not send any data to me or to anyone else on its own. There are no analytics, advertising or tracking services in the app. The contact link in the app opens your email app, the website link opens your browser. The app itself sends nothing.

Children

Worksheets can contain children's names or other details about them. Because the app transmits nothing, this information only leaves your device if you share the file yourself.

Questions

For questions about privacy, write to me at support@sichtnah.com. The general privacy policy applies to this website.
