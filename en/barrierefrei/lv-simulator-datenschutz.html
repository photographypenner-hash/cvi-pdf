LV Simulator Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

In short: LV Simulator collects no data. Everything stays on your device. The app needs no internet and has no account, no ads and no analytics software.

Who is responsible?

Philipp, Zurich
Email: support@sichtnah.com

Camera

The app uses the camera to show you live how your materials may look with a visual impairment. Camera images stay only in your device's memory. They are not saved and not shared. When you close the app, they are gone. You can turn off camera access at any time in your device's settings.

Photos

With “Load photo” you choose one photo from your library yourself. The app only sees this one photo, not your whole library. The photo also stays only in memory.

Share and save

A simulated image is only created when you explicitly tap “Share”. You decide where it goes in the iOS share sheet. Only if you choose “Save Image” there does iOS ask for permission and store the image in your photos.

What the app remembers

The app remembers just one setting on your device: whether you have already seen the introduction on first launch. This setting never leaves your device. It is deleted when you delete the app.

Sharing with others

No data is passed on to me or to anyone else. There are no third-party services in the app.

Children

Please do not capture worksheets with children's names or other details about them. Even though nothing is stored, this way no images with personal data are created in the first place.

Questions

For questions about privacy, write to me at support@sichtnah.com. The general privacy policy applies to this website.
